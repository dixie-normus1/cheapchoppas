About Us

Cheap Choppas is a Kiwi-made website dedicated to the fascinating world of helicopters. Whether you're keen on learning about a classic Bell, a modern Airbus, or a massive military chopper, we've got information on a wide range of helicopter models all in one place. Our goal is to make helicopter information easy to understand and accessible without drowning you in complicated aviation jargon. So, whether you're a full-on aviation enthusiast or just having a geez at some cool choppas, have a poke around and see what takes your fancy.
